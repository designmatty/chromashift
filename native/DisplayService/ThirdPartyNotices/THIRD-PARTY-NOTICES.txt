NvAPIWrapper.dll is a replaceable shared library beside
ChromaShift.DisplayService.exe. You may replace it with a modified build that
keeps the same assembly identity and interface. Close ChromaShift through its
tray Exit action before replacing the file, then put the replacement at:

resources\display-service\NvAPIWrapper.dll
